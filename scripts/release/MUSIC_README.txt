Music mods
==========

Recordings in this folder can replace the games' music: each song plays its
recording instead, looping where the game loops it. Sound effects stay as
they are.

  Music/
    TBS/   The Broken Seal: 1-01 ... 1-43 (FLAC) and loops.txt
    TLA/   The Lost Age:    2-01 ... 2-54 (FLAC) and loops.txt

The Lost Age plays its own folder, and TBS for the songs it borrows from The
Broken Seal (such as the victory fanfare), so keep both.

The first time the launcher finds recordings here, it asks whether to use
them. Change it later with "Enable Music Mod" under the launcher's cogwheel,
or in game under F1 > Audio (switches straight away).

Remastered soundtrack by Vincent Dortel aka Juste-D
---------------------------------------------------
Juste-D's remastered Golden Sun and The Lost Age soundtracks are available
as a ready-made music mod, shared with his permission. Many thanks to him
for the remaster and for his help with the loop points.

  Music mod (both albums, loop points included):
    https://drive.google.com/file/d/1oMiW5pYbjg3Dl9LHHVEYZLpP3jZoryLo/view?usp=drive_link
  Original albums:
    https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Q2MWzb_uSvXp0Z3vWthvErnzM3xyL6Em
  Juste-D on YouTube:
    https://www.youtube.com/@JusteDni

Unzip the music mod so its TBS and TLA folders land in this Music folder.
Each folder's loops.txt holds the loop points for those exact recordings;
keep them together.

Making your own music mod (file names, loop points, the loop finder tool):
