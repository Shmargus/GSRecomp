What is it?

Golden Sun Recompiled turns both Game Boy Advance games into programs that run straight on your PC. It isn't an emulator: the games' own code is translated into PC code, so you get the same adventure with a wider view, a proper settings menu and a few optional extras. You bring your own copy of each game; nothing from the games comes with this project.

The Broken Seal

Isaac's journey, from the storm over Vale to Venus Lighthouse.

The Lost Age

Felix's side of the story, across the Great Eastern Sea to Mars Lighthouse.

One launcher runs both. With Seamless mode the end of The Broken Seal carries straight on into The Lost Age, and The Lost Age can import your Broken Seal team from a save.

Still in testing. Both games are playable from start to finish, but a few things may still look or sound slightly off.

Music mods

Both games can play recordings in place of the original soundtrack. Each song loops exactly where the game loops it; sound effects stay as they are.

  1. Unzip a music mod next to GoldenSunLauncher, into its Music folder.
  2. Start the launcher: it finds the music and asks once whether to use it.
  3. Switch it any time with Enable Music Mod under the cogwheel, or F1 → Audio in game.

The first music mod is the remastered soundtrack of both games by Vincent Dortel aka Juste-D, shared with his permission; many thanks to him, also for his help with the loop points. Not every track has been heard in game yet: if one loops wrongly, please tell us.

FAQ

How do I report a bug?
  1. Press F12 right after the problem. The game keeps the last two seconds, so a flicker that's already gone is still caught.
  2. Close the game. A window asks what happened; write a few words and press Send report.

After a crash the same window opens by itself. Nothing is sent unless you press Send report, and your user name is taken out of the report.

Is this an emulator?

No. The games' own code is translated into PC code ahead of time, so they run directly on your PC instead of through a program that imitates the Game Boy Advance.

Do I need my own copy of the games?

Yes, as USA/Europe game files. The launcher checks each is the exact version this was built for. No BIOS file is needed.

Why does the first start take a few minutes?

The first time, each game is prepared from your game file on your PC, with a progress bar. After that it starts straight away, until an update needs it to prepare again.

How do I update, and do my saves come along?

The launcher tells you when a new version is out and offers its download page. Unzip the new version into a new folder, start its launcher and choose your game file from the old folder's ROMs: your saves and save states come along.

Can I take my Broken Seal team to The Lost Age?

Yes, three ways: Seamless mode (launcher cogwheel) goes straight on after the ending; a new Lost Age game asks for your Broken Seal save file; and F1 → Game → Update from The Broken Seal brings it into an existing Lost Age save, keeping a backup.

How do music mods work?

The recordings go in the Music folder next to the launcher, in TBS and TLA; a ready-made mod already has that layout. The Lost Age also plays the Broken Seal recordings for the songs it borrows from the first game, so keep both. Making your own is explained in MUSIC_MODS.md.

Can I play a translation or a hack?

The launcher's cogwheel has Skip the ROM check for each game. Only the original games are supported, so expect problems.

Is it finished, and does it cost anything?

Not finished yet: test release 0.6 is out, and each release lists what changed and its known issues. It's free, a noncommercial fan project.
