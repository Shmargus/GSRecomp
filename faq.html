Which game files work?

The USA/Europe, Spanish, Italian, French and German versions of each game, plus two patched fan translations: Kyle the Runner's Brazilian Portuguese (The Broken Seal Beta 1.01, The Lost Age Beta 1) and Polish (The Broken Seal v1, The Lost Age 1.1c). A few romhacks work too (see below). Click the flag under the game, then choose the matching file; the launcher checks it and tells you if they don't match. The two games can use different languages.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes, on Android 9 or newer. Install the Android app from the downloads, choose your game file on the phone, and the phone prepares the game itself. See Getting Started. The Djinn and Treasure Trackers are the first page of the phone's menu.

How do I report a bug?
  1. Press F12 right after the problem. The game keeps the last two seconds.
  2. Close the game, write a few words in the window that opens and press Send report.

After a crash the same window opens by itself. Nothing is sent unless you press Send report, and your user name is taken out.

Can I switch language and keep my progress?

Each language has its own saves and savestates. After building a new language, the launcher offers to copy your English in-game save across; you continue from the nearest sanctuary. Savestates don't move between languages.

Is this an emulator?

No. The games' own code is translated into PC code ahead of time, so they run directly on your PC instead of through a program that imitates the Game Boy Advance.

Why does the first start take a few minutes?

The first time, and for each new language, the game is prepared from your game file on your PC, with a progress bar. After that it starts straight away.

How do I update, and do my saves come along?

The launcher tells you when a new version is out. Unzip it into a new folder, start its launcher and choose your game file from the old folder's ROMs folder: your saves and savestates come along.

Can I take my Broken Seal team to The Lost Age?

Yes: Seamless mode (launcher settings) goes straight on after the ending; a new Lost Age game asks for your Broken Seal save; and F1 → Game → Update from The Broken Seal updates an existing Lost Age save, keeping a backup.

Can I play a fan translation or a hack?

The Brazilian Portuguese and Polish translations are supported: patch your ROM first, then pick that flag. Romhacks go through the Romhack button under each cartridge: Golden Sun Reloaded (1.83a for The Broken Seal, 1.83 for The Lost Age), Dawn of Djinn 2.0.5 Casual and Veteran and Jenna Edition 1.0.0 Modern (The Broken Seal), and Maheo Hack 2.0 and Polish Balance Age 1.34hf (The Lost Age). Pick your patched ROM once and it stays in the list; each hack has its own saves. Romhacks are still experimental. For anything else, the launcher settings have Skip the ROM check; it isn't supported, so expect problems.

How do I play a randomized or Challenge run?

Start a New Game and name your hero: a few questions follow, including Randomize and Challenge run. Each randomized run has a Seed you can share; type a friend's Seed at New Game to play the same run. Your Seed and the run sheet are in F1 → Game. Both modes are still experimental, so please send a report (with your Seed) if something goes wrong.

Is it finished, and does it cost anything?

Both games are playable from start to finish. Each release lists its changes and known issues. It's free, a noncommercial fan project.
