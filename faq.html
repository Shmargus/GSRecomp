Which game files work?

The USA/Europe, Spanish, Italian, French and German versions of each game. Click the flag under the game, then choose the matching file; the launcher checks it and tells you if they don't match. The two games can use different languages.

How do I report a bug?
  1. Press F12 right after the problem. The game keeps the last two seconds.
  2. Close the game, write a few words in the window that opens and press Send report.

After a crash the same window opens by itself. Nothing is sent unless you press Send report, and your user name is taken out.

Can I switch language and keep my progress?

Each language has its own saves and savestates. After building a new language, the launcher offers to copy your English in-game save across; you continue from the nearest sanctuary. Savestates don't move between languages.

Is this an emulator?

No. The games' own code is translated into PC code ahead of time, so they run directly on your PC instead of through a program that imitates the Game Boy Advance.

Why does the first start take a few minutes?

The first time, and for each new language, the game is prepared from your game file on your PC, with a progress bar. After that it starts straight away.

How do I update, and do my saves come along?

The launcher tells you when a new version is out. Unzip it into a new folder, start its launcher and choose your game file from the old folder's ROMs folder: your saves and savestates come along.

Can I take my Broken Seal team to The Lost Age?

Yes: Seamless mode (launcher settings) goes straight on after the ending; a new Lost Age game asks for your Broken Seal save; and F1 → Game → Update from The Broken Seal updates an existing Lost Age save, keeping a backup.

Can I play a fan translation or a hack?

The official Spanish, Italian, French and German versions are supported. For anything else, the launcher settings have Skip the ROM check; it isn't supported, so expect problems.

Is it finished, and does it cost anything?

Both games are playable from start to finish. Each release lists its changes and known issues. It's free, a noncommercial fan project.
