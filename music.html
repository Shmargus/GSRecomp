Music mods

Play recordings in place of the original soundtrack, looping exactly where the game loops each song.

  1. Unzip a music mod into the Music folder next to GoldenSunLauncher.
  2. Start the launcher; it asks once whether to use it.
  3. Switch it any time in the launcher settings or F1 → Audio.

The remastered soundtrack of both games is by Vincent Dortel aka Juste-D, shared with his permission. Many thanks to him.

Track swaps

  1. Load a game and open F1 → Music.
  2. Turn on Enable area music choices.
  3. Search for an area and choose a track. Battle music has its own list.

Use the dice to shuffle one entry, or Randomize all to shuffle the soundtrack. Reset all restores the original choices.
